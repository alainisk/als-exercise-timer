Privacy

Accounts and workouts

Firebase Authentication manages your username-associated account, email and password. Your password is sent over HTTPS for authentication and is not saved in the workout database. Your email supports password recovery. The app stores session credentials in your browser so you can stay logged in.

Your private workout library and sharing permissions are stored in Firebase Realtime Database. The Railway API verifies your account before reading or changing them. Profiles organize workouts inside your account; they are not separate user accounts. Family links are no longer used for access.

Videos and images

Uploaded files are stored in a private Railway bucket. Account files use server access controls rather than the former family-link encryption. The service operator and hosting providers can process stored content. Downloads use signed URLs valid for one minute. Files you download into your own library can be kept in browser storage for offline use.

YouTube and Vimeo links store the source URL rather than a copy of the video. Embedded players connect directly to those providers, which receive connection information under their own privacy policies. They require internet and may be unavailable if the provider restricts embedding or removes the video.

Sharing

Sharing by username gives that account permission to view and run the selected workout and make an independent copy. The recipient sees your username. Only you can change the original workout or its recipient list. Removing a person stops new authorized access; a previously issued download URL can remain usable for up to one minute. Copies, links or files they already saved cannot be recalled.

Your controls

Deleting a workout or profile removes it from the active library after syncing and removes its sharing permissions. Uploaded cloud files are retained to protect other copies and are not automatically erased. Contact us for account or retained-file deletion.

Logging out first saves your changes, then removes this account’s offline library, cached files and session from this browser. Original device libraries and older family libraries remain separate for recovery. Import device workouts copies locally available workouts and files into your account without changing the originals. Clearing this website’s browser data removes local copies.

The app does not use advertising or sell your data. Hosting providers process connection information under their policies. Questions: alainisk@gmail.com.
