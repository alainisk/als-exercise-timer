Privacy

Tabata Timer stores workout profiles, workout details and downloaded media in your browser. Profiles are convenience spaces, not password-protected accounts.

Optional family sync

Creating a family link stores encrypted profile and workout details, including media file references, in Firebase. The secret in the link decrypts this library. Anyone with the complete family link can read and change its workout information. Keep it private. Original device libraries remain separate.

Optional Google Drive connection

The app requests permission to access only Google Drive files used with this app. Uploaded videos and images are saved in a Tabata Timer Media folder in your Google Drive and count toward your account storage. They are protected by Google Drive permissions; they are not encrypted with your family link key. A family link alone does not grant access to private Drive files.

Google access tokens remain in page memory and are not stored in Firebase or included in family links. An account identifier is stored locally to distinguish cached upload references. Downloaded files stay in browser storage for offline use. Reconnect Google Drive after reloading or when access expires.

Your controls

You can disconnect Drive in the Family link dialog and revoke the app’s permission in your Google Account. Deleting workouts or profiles removes them from the family library when synced; it does not delete uploaded Drive files or all browser caches. Manage uploaded files in Google Drive and clear browser site data to remove local copies. Leaving a family library does not delete its cloud record or invalidate copies of the link.

The app does not use advertising or sell your data. Google services process connection information according to their policies. Questions: alainisk@gmail.com.
