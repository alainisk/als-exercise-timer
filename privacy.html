Privacy

Tabata Timer stores workout profiles, workout details and downloaded media in your browser. Profiles are convenience spaces, not password-protected accounts.

Private family sync

Creating a family link stores encrypted profiles, workout details and media in a private Railway storage bucket. Your browser encrypts content using the secret in the link. The server receives a separate derived access token, not the encryption key. Anyone with the complete family link can read and change the library. Keep it private.

Media downloads use temporary signed URLs to encrypted files. Downloaded files remain in browser storage for offline use. A maximum of 512 MB per file applies. Uploads interrupted before completion may need to restart.

Migration and Google Drive

Older Firebase family libraries are copied to Railway when opened. The original Firebase record and device libraries are retained for recovery. If older media was stored in Google Drive and is no longer cached locally, reconnect that Google account using Recover older Google Drive media. Google access is restricted to files used with this app. Tokens remain in page memory and are never included in family links or uploaded to Railway.

Your controls

Deleting workouts or profiles removes them from the active library when synced. It does not automatically erase retained cloud media, older Firebase copies or browser caches. Clear browser site data to remove local copies. Leaving a family library does not delete its cloud record or invalidate copies of the link. Google access can be revoked in your Google Account.

The app does not use advertising or sell your data. Hosting providers process connection information under their policies. Questions: alainisk@gmail.com.
